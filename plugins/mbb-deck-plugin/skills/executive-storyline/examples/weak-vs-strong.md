# Example: the same answer, bottom-up and top-down

Question from the managing director in a corridor: "Where do we stand on the warehouse site decision?"

## Weak answer

> "It has been an intense few weeks. We started with the property agents, then Operations sent their volume forecast, which changed the picture, so we redid the comparison. We also visited two of the sites and spoke to the works council. Right now the northern site looks reasonably good, but we are not quite finished."

What is wrong:

- **Process first.** Four of five sentences report activity.
- **Governing thought buried and hedged.** It arrives last, as "looks reasonably good".
- **No structure.** The listener cannot tell how many reasons there are or which matters most.
- **No ask.** The answer ends on the team's state, not on what happens next.

## Strong answer

> "Our current hypothesis is the northern site, for three reasons. First, it is the only one that covers next-day delivery for the whole region. Second, it is available from January, which the other two are not. Third, the lease runs on terms we already have at the southern warehouse. As a next step we confirm the transport cost with the two carriers, and we bring the decision to you in the April meeting."

What changed:

- **Governing thought first,** stated as a hypothesis because the work is not finished. Hedging the status of the conclusion is honest; hedging the conclusion itself is not.
- **Three content arguments,** announced as three and ordered by importance.
- **Process only in the close,** as the next step, with the date the decision comes back.

## The same move in writing

Weak subject line and opening:

> Subject: Update warehouse project
>
> Dear all, as discussed, we have continued our analysis over the past weeks and would like to share where we are...

Strong:

> Subject: Decision needed in the April meeting: northern site for the new warehouse
>
> **Recommendation: lease the northern site from January.**
>
> - **Coverage** - the only site that reaches the whole region next day
> - **Availability** - free from January; the other two are not
> - **Terms** - the lease matches the one we hold at the southern warehouse
>
> Open point: transport cost, confirmed with both carriers before the meeting.

The example gives no figures because the brief gave none. In a real draft each bullet carries its number.
